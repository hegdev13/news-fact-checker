By Arun Kumar

India's economy grew 7.8% in the most recent quarter, retaining its position as
the fastest-growing major economy, government data released on Friday showed.

The expansion, up from 6.3% in the previous quarter, was driven by a strong
services sector and a rebound in farm output after a good monsoon. Officials said
the figure put India comfortably ahead of China, which grew at a slower pace over
the same period.

Economists cautioned that a low GDP deflator had flattered the headline number,
and that private investment and urban consumption remained soft. Even so, most
analysts said the underlying momentum of the economy was intact heading into the
festival season.

The finance ministry said the data reflected "the resilience of the Indian growth
story" and projected that full-year growth would stay above 7%.
